Club Sandwich

It's time for a deli special... the club sandwich

Ingredients

  1. Bread
  2. Lettuce
  3. Tomato
  4. Chicken
  5. Bacon
  6. Mayonnaise
  7. Black pepper
  8. Salt

Steps

  1. Gather all your ingredients.
  2. Cook the bacon in the pan, under the grill or which ever way you like.
  3. Same goes with the chicken.
  4. Slice the tomato.
  5. Toast the bread (3 slices) and butter it.
  6. Time to assemble. Take the first layer and place a layer of lettuce. Then add the chicken with a sprinkle of salt & pepper. Next its time for the middle layer of toast. On that layer add the bacon and tomoato, then close the lid with the final slice of toast.
