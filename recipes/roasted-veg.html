Easy Roasted Veg Recipe

No time? No problem with this easy to follow and delicious roasted veg recipe

Ingredients

  1. Lazy Garlic
  2. Coliflower
  3. Parsley
  4. Salt
  5. Pepper

Steps

  1. Gather all your ingredients.
  2. Clean the coliflower
  3. Rub two teaspoons of lazy garlic on the coliflower
  4. Sprinkle salt, pepper and parsley
  5. Pop it in the over at 160 for 40mins
  6. Finish off with garnish of your choice, a little cheese maybe? Go on..
